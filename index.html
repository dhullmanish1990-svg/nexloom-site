How it works

1

Set the brief

Target price, tolerance, deadline, rounds, tone and up to three negotiation levers.

2

NexLoom negotiates

It writes the first email to each supplier, reads every reply and sends a counter, from your own mailbox.

3

Suppliers just reply

No portal, no login, no integration. Prices and terms are extracted and compared for you.

4

You decide

Target met, odd price, legal terms, final winner: the agent stops and asks you.

Built for buyers who need control, not a black box

Agent or human, per supplier

Switch any supplier between automatic and manual. Pause, resume or stop an RFQ or a single supplier.

A kill switch per RFQ

One switch turns the agent off for every supplier at once and makes the whole conversation manual.

Human checkpoints

Target reached, price jumps, legal or payment-security terms, supplier questions, low confidence, failed guardrail and more.

Cannot leak your position

A guardrail blocks any draft that reveals your target, your budget or another supplier's offer.

Locked once started

RFQ settings lock when it starts. Only an admin can change them, with a logged reason.

Clear results

Price trends, supplier phases, round-by-round levers, stop reasons, ranking and a CSV export for every RFQ.

Compliance and trust from day one

Your data stays yours

Every customer's data is isolated in the database with row-level security. A full audit log records every action.

EU-ready

Designed for EU hosting and GDPR. AI disclosure aligned with EU AI Act Article 50 is on our roadmap.

No surprise emails

A send gate checks RFQ status, deadline and recipient before every email, and each message is sent at most once.

Transparent to suppliers

A human can take over any conversation at any time.
